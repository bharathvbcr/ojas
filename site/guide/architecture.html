Architecture & crates

Dependencies point down. Rust programs use the model and backend layers directly; Go and C hosts enter through the C-ABI engine.

Test counts are from cargo test --workspace --release and the Go suite on 2026-10-01.

ojas-core37 passed

Tensor views with strides and byte offsets, the memory budget, the dtype system, and checked shape limits.

ojas-cpu89 passed

Reference CPU backend: Exact packed GEMM on a persistent pool (1 to 256 threads), Fast tier via Accelerate, Muon NS5 and AdamW.

ojas-simd24 passed

Fast-tier GEMM kernels for ARM NEON and x86_64 AVX2+FMA, and the Apple Accelerate wrapper.

ojas-metal66 passed

Device-resident Metal 4 training step on Apple Silicon through tessl GEMM and MSL kernels.

ojas-wgpu45 passed

Portable WGSL compute on WebGPU with device-resident tensors, including Muon NS5.

ojas-kernels8 passed

Hardware-agnostic WGSL/CUDA sources, launch-grid geometry, and the parity harness.

ojas-autograd28 passed

Reverse-mode tape with device residency, checked against f64 central-difference gradcheck.

ojas-io36 passed

Strict safetensors (F32, BF16, F16, I64, U16) and the Checkpoint v1 codec; refuses mutated headers and overlapping ranges.

ojas-data21 passed

TokenBin reader, seeded epoch batch sampler, counter-based RNG, and GPT-2 BPE.

ojas-infer14 passed

KV cache and greedy decode on the CPU, seeded temperature/top-k/top-p sampling, and logit validation.

ojas-capi41 passed

The engine behind the Go package: session store, panic boundary, and device routing.

go/ (ojas SDK)27 passed

In-process Go client: Load, LoadOn, Step, Generate, GenerateGreedy, Free, and Close via libgusset.a.

ojas-device18 passed

Device enumeration, host CPU probing, and ResourcePolicy planning, with refusal instead of fallback.

ojas-oracle6 passed

Golden numerical fixtures computed in f64 for offline verification.

ojas-cuda & ojas-hip8 + 8 passed

NVIDIA CUDA and AMD HIP probes behind the cuda and hip features; the default build runs no device kernel.
